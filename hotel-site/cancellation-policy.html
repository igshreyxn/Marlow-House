Cancellation & Refund Policy

We know plans change. Here's exactly what happens if yours do.

Refund tiers

CancelledRefund
48 hours or more before check-in100% refund
24–48 hours before check-in50% refund
Within 24 hours of check-in, or no-showNo refund

How refunds are processed

  • Refunds are issued to your original payment method.
  • Processing typically takes 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.
  • You'll receive an email confirming the refund amount once it's initiated.

How to cancel

  • Sign in to your account and go to My Bookings.
  • Select the booking and choose Cancel Booking — the applicable refund amount is shown before you confirm.
  • If you're unable to access your account, contact us at stay@marlowhouse.example or +91 00000 00000.

Exceptions

Special rates, promotional bookings, or extended-stay reservations may carry different terms — these will always be shown clearly at the time of booking, before payment.
